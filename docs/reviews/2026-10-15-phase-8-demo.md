**Visibility.** The owner made the repository public before this record was made. PROJECT.md §6

### Secret scan of the whole history (task 7.1)

- **Command:** every object from `git rev-list --objects --all` was written out with
  `git cat-file blob` into a scratch folder, one file per blob (808 blobs). `detect-secrets scan
  --all-files .` then ran from inside that folder.
- **Proof the scan works:** a planted test key and a known hash file were both found. A first
  run, given the folder's absolute path, found nothing at all, the planted key included, so it
  was discarded.
- **Result:**
  - No secret appears.
  - The only findings are hashes: SHA-256 digests in `docs/brief/` and the skill source manifest,
    and the SHA-512 integrity values in `package-lock.json`.
  - A search of every blob for API-key shapes found none: Anthropic `sk-ant-`, `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY=`,
    an `x-api-key` setting (as 21st.dev uses), GitHub `ghp_` and `github_pat_`, private-key
    headers and AWS key IDs.
  - `.env` and `.codex/config.toml` were never committed.

### Survey for figures from collected prices (task 7.2, D26)

- **Method:** read-only, from the two local databases of collected prices.
  - **Inputs:** every distinct collected price (4,579) and every distinct USD/GPU-hour (584).
  - **Formats:** each value as stored, at 4 decimals, at 2 decimals and with thousands
    separators, kept when it has at least 4 significant digits.
  - **Derived figures:** the workload figures the dashboard and brief would derive from each
    USD/GPU-hour (32 GPUs × 720 hours, and the +25% and +50% contingencies), kept when they have
    at least 5 significant digits.
  - **Search:** 11,903 strings, as exact whole numbers in every history blob and every tracked
    file. The output showed counts, paths and each match's context with the number masked, never
    a price.
- **Result:** no live Azure price, cost, exposure or SKU-level price figure appears in the tracked
  files or the history. Every match was a coincidence:
  - invented demo figures that happen to equal figures derived from collected prices (the demo's
    costs and contingencies, in the brief, specs, tests and DESIGN.md);
  - fragments of SHA-256 digests and commit IDs;
  - timestamps and sizes in `uv.lock`;
  - coordinates in the architecture SVGs;
  - colour-contrast ratios in DESIGN.md.
- **Limit:** a collected price written in another format, or a round number that is also an
  invented one, would not be told apart by this search.
- **Present, as D26 records:** aggregate counts and coarse distributions from the one local
  snapshot, in design and review records and HANDOFF.
